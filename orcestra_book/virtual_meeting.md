# Virtual ORCESTRA Meetings

```{admonition} Mailing list
:class: tip
To receive the meeting link and announcements of future seminars, [click here](https://listes.services.cnrs.fr/wws/subscribe/orcestra-virtual-meeting?previous_action=subscribe), and then look for the confirmation email. The meeting link will be shared a few hours before the meeting.

```

To stay connected within the ORCESTRA community, we meet virtually every month. Our goal is to encourage the sharing of datasets and ideas across the ORCESTRA community. We welcome presentations of work in progress and encourage questions, suggestions, and open discussion throughout. The usual format will be two speakers, each with 20 minutes to present and 10 minutes for questions and discussion.
